Zucchini Lasagna

Home

Description

Zucchini lasagna is a modern twist on regular lasagna! It is made with layers of baked zucchini instead of pasta noodles. Between each layer are all the classics of regular lasagna that we know and love. cheesy ricotta filling, fresh basil, and rich marinara!

This low-carb lasagna is made with zucchini slices instead of lasagna noodles for a hearty and satisfying gluten-free beef lasagna. It's delicious and a real crowd-pleaser!

Ingredients

  • Cooking spray
  • Zucchinis: 1 ½ large zucchinis thinly sliced lengthwise
  • Olive oil: 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Ground beef: 1 pound ground beef
  • Marinara sauce: 1 ½ cups low-carb marinara sauce
  • Salt: 2 teaspoons salt, divided
  • Dried oregano: 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Ground black pepper: ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • Ricotta cheese: 1 (8 ounce) container ricotta cheese
  • Egg: 1 large egg
  • Ground nutmeg: ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • Mozzarela cheese: 2 cups shredded mozzarela cheese
  • Parmesan cheese: ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Aluminium foil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven & grease: Preheat the oven to 375℉ (190℃). Grease an 9-inch square baking dish with cooking spray.
  2. Pat & set aside: Pat dry zucchini slices with a paper towel to remove excess moisture. Set aside.
  3. Heat oil & cook: Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium-high heat. Add ground beef; cook until browned, 5 to 8 minutes.
